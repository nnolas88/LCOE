# LCOE
# U.S. Renewable Energy LCOE Analysis

## Project Overview
This project compares the projected Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE) of utility-scale solar PV and land-based wind in the United States using data from the NREL 2024 Annual Technology Baseline (ATB).

Using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib, I analyzed capital expenditure (CAPEX), fixed operating and maintenance costs, capacity factors, and LCOE under a consistent set of assumptions for 2027.

## Key Findings
- **Land-based wind LCOE:** $14.53/MWh
- **Utility-scale solar PV LCOE:** $24.94/MWh
- Wind's projected LCOE was approximately 41.7% lower than solar's under the selected assumptions.
- Sensitivity analysis showed that reducing the cost recovery period from 30 years to 20 years increased LCOE by 11.22% for wind and 17.84% for solar.

## Tools & Skills
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis, Energy Economics, LCOE Analysis, Sensitivity Analysis, Data Visualization.

## Data Source
National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL), 2024 Annual Technology Baseline.

## Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates how capital costs, capacity factors, and financial assumptions influence the estimated cost of electricity generation. The results apply to the selected technologies and assumptions and do not represent a complete assessment of electricity market competitiveness.
